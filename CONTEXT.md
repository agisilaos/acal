# Calendar automation

acal works with calendar events and their scheduled occurrences.

## Language

**Recurring series**:
A set of scheduled occurrences generated by a repeating rule, including occurrences edited independently of the rest.
_Avoid_: Repeating event when the distinction between a series and one occurrence matters.

**Occurrence**:
One scheduled instance of an event, whether independent or part of a recurring series.
_Avoid_: Series when referring to one scheduled instance.

**Detached occurrence**:
An occurrence of a recurring series edited independently of the remaining occurrences.
_Avoid_: Independent event when the occurrence still belongs to a recurring series.
